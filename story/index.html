A little about me

Still curious. Still figuring it out.

I’m Mayank. I like technology, ideas, people, and seeing what happens when I try something unfamiliar.

AI, automation, and building things for the web are a big part of what I do. I’m also curious about people, creativity, and ideas outside the work itself. I enjoy a conversation that opens up something I hadn’t considered.
