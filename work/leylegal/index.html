Leylegal

Legal research for Indian advocates and law students, in English, Hindi, and Hinglish.

Visit live website ↗View GitHub repository ↗

The project.

Leylegal is the project I’m most proud of so far. It brings section lookups, judgment research, and case-status checks into one browser-based experience for advocates and law students.

What it brings together

  1. 01

    Sections across criminal codes

    Look up provisions and their counterparts across IPC / BNS and CrPC / BNSS.

  2. 02

    Judgment research

    Search for relevant judgments with links to the full text.

  3. 03

    Case status

    Check a matter by its CNR number.

  4. 04

    Multilingual access

    Ask questions in Hindi, English, or Hinglish.

Leylegal is in early access. Research outputs require verification against original legal sources.
