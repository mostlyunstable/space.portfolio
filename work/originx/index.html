OriginX

Digital product passports and verifiable product lifecycles, accessible through a QR code.

View GitHub repository ↗

The project.

With OriginX, I’ve been working on product identity and provenance: giving a physical product a digital passport and a history that can be checked. The project has separate admin, organization, and consumer views connected to a shared API.

What it brings together

  1. 01

    Digital product passports

    A verifiable identity for physical products and their lifecycle records.

  2. 02

    Tamper-evident history

    SHA-256-linked events and EVM blockchain anchoring support verification.

  3. 03

    Supply-chain workflows

    Custody transfers, inventory, shipments, incidents, and recalls.

  4. 04

    Public verification

    QR-based consumer access to product verification.

Technology

React · Node.js · PostgreSQL / Supabase · Solidity / EVM · Docker
