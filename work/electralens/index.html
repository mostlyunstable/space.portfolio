ElectraLens

Electoral-roll comparison, statistical signals for human review, and verifiable evidence.

View GitHub repository ↗

The project.

ElectraLens is my civic-tech project for exploring changes in electoral rolls. It compares revisions, explains statistical findings, and lets people verify evidence. A pattern worth looking at isn’t proof of wrongdoing; the findings are there for people to review.

What it brings together

  1. 01

    Revision comparisons

    Inspect additions, deletions, and corrections between roll versions.

  2. 02

    Human-review signals

    Surface statistical anomalies and explain findings in plain language.

  3. 03

    Evidence verification

    Browser-based cryptographic checks for evidence manifests.

  4. 04

    Accessible reporting

    English and Hindi support, with printable audit reports.

Technology

Next.js · TypeScript · Fastify · Python · Supabase · Solidity · Three.js

Independent project, not affiliated with the Election Commission of India. Demonstration data is synthetic; anomalies require human review.
